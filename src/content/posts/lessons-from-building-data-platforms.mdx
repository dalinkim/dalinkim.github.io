## 1. Your users are other engineers

A platform succeeds when the people building on it can move without asking you for help. Good defaults and clear docs beat clever features.

## 2. Make the paved road the easy road

If the recommended way to build a pipeline is harder than the hacky way, people will take the hacky way. Every time.

## 3. Observability is a feature

Lineage, freshness and quality checks aren't extras. They're how your users learn to trust the data.
